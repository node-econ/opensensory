Open Sensory

Open Sensory builds room-aware hearing technology that helps people hear more clearly in noisy spaces where hearing loss is hardest to manage, by working with a room's own acoustic properties instead of fighting them.

Our story

We coined the name Open Sensory in 2020 for an automatic sensor system for agricultural pest monitoring. That work was interrupted by the pandemic, but the underlying research continued under a new venture, Resight Laboratories. Open Sensory went dormant for a couple of years while that work continued under its own name.

In 2025, after winding down Resight Laboratories, we relaunched Open Sensory, now as the home for our emerging hearing technologies.

The thread running through all of this is a focus on embedded systems and connected sensing, and everything we've learned building these advanced systems.

What we're building now

Our focus is on assistive-listening infrastructure — with a broader vision for personal hearing technology. If you would like to learn more, please contact us.

A principle we hold to

Open Sensory stays focused on one thing: the listener's experience of sound. We're not building a general wellness-wearable platform that happens to start with ears — just technology that helps people hear what they want to hear.
